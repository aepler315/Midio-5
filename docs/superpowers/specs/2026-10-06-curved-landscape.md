# Curved landscape rebuild

The rejected circular scene showed a smooth foreground cap with mountains, lake and cast squeezed into its edge. Rebuild the visible world as a continuous spherical landscape. Keep deterministic automatic travel and the stellar background.

Use longitude `x / 1800` and latitude `z * .30 / 1800` to project the terrain onto a sphere. Carry altitude along its full three-dimensional radial normal. The near ground reaches the front pole and closes naturally; a sphere eight metres below sea level backs the terrain instead of a protruding cap. Water, props, characters and foot contacts use the same surface and bases. Default noncircular consumers retain their flat behavior.

Stage the ordinary camera obliquely over the upper surface so the lake has visible area and the grounded performer is around 7% of landscape picture height. Increase the circular cast by 1.6 and compact their excursions. The opening briefly establishes the round world; musical pullbacks retain the inhabited landscape instead of retreating to planetary scale. Keep camera orientation steady and retain seek/reduced-motion behavior.

Before publication, inspect actual application frames in landscape and portrait plus a ten-second motion sample. Review visible route width, connected land/water, performer readability and foot contact, and the absence of the blank cap. Mechanical tests separately cover spherical projection, seam closure, radial clipping, camera clearance and context recovery. Do not label visual quality as proven by those tests.
