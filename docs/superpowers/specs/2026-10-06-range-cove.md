# Range: residents of a moonlit cove

The floating dock made the trio read as performers on a stage. The approved replacement places them in Muncho's geographic shoreline, in an asymmetric triangle at different real depths.

Broshi rests on wet gravel, with fixed ground contact and modest leaning. Midio sits partly underwater among reeds; the lake cuts its lower silhouette and owns the reflection and wake. Midasus drifts behind them near a pine, accompanied by small stars. Retain the recognizable angular mesh kit as shallow faceted bodies, with fixed world orientation, ambient shading and restrained luminous edges.

A lower, closer lateral camera makes the residents readable without turning them into screen overlays. Terrain, rocks, reeds, trees, residents and reflections share projection and depth. Dressing is sampled against shipped terrain and never makes a shared pedestal. Only the validated Muncho cove receives the habitat; explicit other views retain their landscapes.

Music comes from the existing canonical heard-time history and source handoff. Bass presses into water near Broshi; rhythmic contacts radiate from Midio's water; melody guides Midasus and its companions. Body gestures are slow and independent, with no synchronized hops or scale pulses. Silence leaves residents present and still. Seeking reconstructs the same poses. Reduced motion freezes movement, and reduced flash limits light modulation.

Keep the existing dense stars and sunset → moonlight → sunrise cycle. Preserve scenery-only Range and other worlds. Remove the Canvas dock, glyph overlay and fabricated reflection layer. Reserve and release habitat GPU resources through RangeScene's existing lifecycle.

Verification includes terrain and water anchor checks, camera clearance and visibility, canonical music and accessibility tests, renderer ownership and disposal, and actual browser captures at sunset, moonlight, sunrise and portrait.
