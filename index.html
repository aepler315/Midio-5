Or just play a song

Put anything on — Spotify, YouTube, whatever you already use — and tap Listen. Midio builds the world out of what it hears.

Uses your microphone, so the music needs to be coming from a speaker rather than headphones. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. A listen runs about three and a half minutes — it can’t know how long your song is, so it plays an arc that length and then ends.

Contains flashing lights, camera shake, and sudden scene cuts. Press R to dampen all three; it turns on automatically if your system asks for reduced motion.
